How to read the result

CLAIMED on a vendor-specific interface is the interesting result: a bridge-free T265 path may be possible.

PROTECTED is expected for USB Video (0x0E) and HID (0x03) interfaces because Chromium deliberately blocks normal WebUSB pages from claiming those classes.

FAILED can mean the operating-system driver owns that interface, the device cannot be opened through WebUSB, or another USB access restriction applies.
